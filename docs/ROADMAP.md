# Roadmap

Current direction, 2026-09-23. The core remains scripted while we learn how to
make pawn behavior grounded, deliberate and understandable. A live core is a
future milestone, not the next dependency.

## Now: meaning and breadth

- Self-describing model perspectives: clear need scales, unknown values and
  contextual choice effects; shorter shared instructions without moving consent
  enforcement into prompts. See [semantic contract](MODEL_PERSPECTIVE.md).
- A frozen snapshot bank spanning need-state contrasts, social strain, limited
  supplies and recovery. Small predetermined repetitions through Claude and Luna;
  score structural validity and grounding separately, with refusals allowed.
- Keep real-game tests for integration, timing, native behavior and persistence.
  Offline evaluations complement rather than replace them.

## Next: broader embodied pawn behavior

- Keep corrected v2 needs and the matched-outlook bank separate from old evidence.
  Future comparisons must validate full canonical inputs and use fresh finite caps.
- Build on the bounded addressed exchange with tests of how recipients interpret
  uncertain or disputed speech. Keep attributed statements distinct from native
  facts, private outlook revisions and permission to act; see [social contract](SOCIAL_EXCHANGE.md).
  The first [offline interpretation check](SPEECH_CHECKS.md) and optional
  [received-message outlook retention](PRIVATE_OUTLOOK.md#received-message-evidence)
  are complete; the [native retention protocol](NATIVE_RETENTION.md) now checks
  the citation path with equal reflection menus, paired restore and fresh consent.
- Test durable outlook reuse without treating a later acceptance as proof of
  persuasion. The small offline message/no-message contrast is complete; next
  build on the [bounded provider diagnostics and name labels](PROVIDER_DIAGNOSTICS.md).
  The [frozen indirect-message pair](INDIRECT_MESSAGE.md) is complete: a statement
  of interest without an explicit request reached a later applied refusal. Next is
  correction or removal after a later message, under a separate finite protocol. Do not count a raw rejected answer as an applied choice. Retention plus recent correspondence does not isolate
  the note's influence. No further allowance is enabled by this roadmap.
  Native chatter stays distinct from coordinator-mediated speech.
- Reassess legibility before adding entry kinds. One fresh-context model reader
  of a retained exported log is complete, but its packet omitted UI/report-header
  framing. One human screenshot read recovered the main sequence; this is limited
  feedback, not general usability validation. Defer UI polish while building the
  foundation; do not infer a UI defect from packet-specific ambiguity.
- Improve grounding coverage only against a separately versioned, held-out set.
  The first numeric/phrase checker deliberately leaves prose unscored and misses
  paraphrases; source IDs alone do not establish support. Keep predictions separate
  from contradictory state claims and never use the score to veto valid choices.
  See [grounding and matched-outlook limits](GROUNDING_CHECKS.md).

## Later, when the pawns are ready

- A bounded live core proposing grounded work and answering requests, with no
  consent bypass. No live-core allowance is enabled by this plan.
- Human-to-core suggestions, pawn correspondence and situated comms-console chat;
  see [playable direction](PLAYABLE_DIRECTION.md).
- Broader actions/campaign content, larger comparative evaluations and unattended
  operation only after their own scope and spending decisions.

## Maintenance policy

The trial-budget table is centralized with historical policy identities preserved.
New evaluation code lives in `trials/`; migrating old native runners is deferred
until there is a concrete benefit. Provider failures retain bounded stage labels
without exposing raw diagnostics to pawns. Keep this roadmap forward-looking;
[historical checkpoints](HISTORY.md), [acceptance evidence](ACCEPTANCE.md) and the
[diary](https://rimworld-concord.pages.dev/) hold past results.
